评估Similarweb的利润和人工智能增长里程碑

看多者认为，Similarweb 可以将其不断扩大的人工智能驱动的数据平台转化为持久的、盈利的增长。最新季度给出了一些具体的证据。 7720 万美元的收入超出了指导，管理层实现了首次正的 GAAP 运营利润，加上 870 万美元的正常化自由现金流，这支持了运营杠杆开始出现在损益表和现金流中的说法。

该论文还严重依赖于与人工智能需求相关的大型、经常性企业交易。在这一领域，里程碑看起来是一致的。 AI相关收入达到总收入的13%。多年合同占 ARR 的 66%，ARR 10 万美元以上的客户目前约占业务的 70%。这一较大群体的净收入保留率为 107%，加上剩余履约义务增长了 26%，达到 3.45 亿美元，这支持了 Sametime 正在深化其企业和人工智能足迹并实现货币化的观点。

Sameweb 熊市关注客户增长放缓

悲观的观点是，Similarweb 的企业推动和人工智能故事掩盖了数据访问和客户依赖方面的结构性风险，而真正的需求广度却停滞不前。最新季度对这一点提出了部分挑战。较大的客户看起来很健康，每个账户的 ARR 超过 25,000 美元和 100,000 美元的群体强劲增长，最大客户的净收入保留率为 107%。这消除了人们对直接定价或高端客户流失压力的担忧。

然而，仍然错过了一些里程碑。 ARR 超过 25,000 美元的客户数量几乎没有变化，为 1,815 人，而一年前为 1,809 人。这支持了这样一种担忧，即增长在很大程度上依赖于推销现有企业，而不是大规模赢得新企业。目前，人工智能相关收入占总收入的 13%，该产品正在寻找用例，但中端市场客户群持平，仍然存在需求集中而非广泛的风险。

Similarweb shares barely flinched after earnings, closing up 2.5% around US$9.01, yet the report itself was anything but muted. The stock had already run hard over the past quarter, so today’s move indicates that investors are still deciding whether this is just another growth story or the start of a profitability reset. The headline is simple: Similarweb posted Q2 revenue of US$77.2m and, crucially for a software data platform, reported a positive GAAP operating profit along with US$8.7m of normalized free cash flow. The market is weighing that margin improvement against the already higher share price. Bulls argue Similarweb can turn its expanding AI driven data platform into durable, profitable growth. The latest quarter gives some concrete proof points. Revenue of US$77.2m beat guidance and management delivered the first positive GAAP operating profit plus US$8.7m of normalized free cash flow, which supports the claim that operating leverage is starting to show up in the income statement and cash flow. The thesis also leans heavily on larger, recurring enterprise deals tied to AI demand. In this area, milestones look aligned. AI related revenue reached 13% of total revenue. Multiyear contracts account for 66% of ARR and customers above US$100k ARR now make up about 70% of the business. Net revenue retention at 107% for this larger cohort, combined with 26% growth in remaining performance obligations to US$345m, supports the view that Similarweb is deepening and monetizing its enterprise and AI footprint. The bearish view is that Similarweb’s enterprise push and AI story mask structural risks in data access and customer reliance, while true breadth of demand stalls. The latest quarter challenges part of that. Larger customers look healthy, with ARR per account for cohorts above US$25,000 and US$100,000 rising strongly and net revenue retention at 107% for the biggest clients. That works against fears of immediate pricing or churn pressure at the top end. However, some milestones are still missed. Customer count above US$25,000 ARR barely moved, at 1,815 versus 1,809 a year ago. That supports the concern that growth leans heavily on upselling existing enterprises rather than winning new ones at scale. With AI related revenue now 13% of total, the product is finding use cases, yet the flat mid market customer base keeps the risk alive that demand is concentrated, not broad based.

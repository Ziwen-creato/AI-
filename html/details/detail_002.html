美国联邦贸易委员会严重打击人工智能副业诈骗，损失高达7400万美元

美国2026-08-12T15:38:23生成于 2026-08-13 15:37

美国联邦贸易委员会一直在开展一项名为“AI Comply 行动”的执法行动，目标是承诺从人工智能中轻松获得被动收入的商业机会计划。根据机构备案文件，与欺骗性人工智能和电子商务宣传相关的案件已消除了超过 7400 万美元的消费者损失。

目标正是那些阅读该网站的人，有志于寻找副业的单身赚钱者。这使得联邦贸易委员会的行动成为你在将资金交给任何为你服务的人工智能企业之前要避免的陷阱的有用地图。

美国联邦贸易委员会行动的目标是什么

这次扫荡针对的是那些将人工智能营销为保证收入捷径的业务，通常是通过自动化在线店面或转售计划。在一个案例中，FTC 表示，一家以 Ascend Ecom 等名义运营的商业机会业务承诺每月提供数千美元的被动收入，欺骗了消费者至少 2500 万美元。

另一项行动导致对一家名为 Air AI 的公司做出 1800 万美元的判决和禁令，该机构称该公司声称其技术可以取代人类销售人员，并帮助人们在几天内赚取数万美元。其中一些计划的个人损失达到六位数。

为什么这对个体经营者读者很重要

围绕人工智能工具构建的副业正在蓬勃发展，而且大多数都是合法的。这种增长也掩盖了不良行为者，因为真实的趋势使虚假的宣传更容易被相信。

对于自由职业者或首次个体创业者来说，损失不仅仅是预付费用。这些计划常常迫使人们寻求商业贷款、信贷额度或库存购买，这可能会让潜在的企业家在赚到一美元之前就负债累累。

自雇读者下一步应该做什么

将任何有保证的收入或自动化人工智能业务视为危险信号，尤其是当它涉及高压销售策略和高昂的前期成本时。联邦贸易委员会在这些案件中的共同点是大胆的收入承诺、夸大的推荐以及在任何实际收入出现之前支付的费用。

付款前先自己算算。以书面形式要求提供可验证的收入声明，在“投诉”和“联邦贸易委员会”等词旁边搜索公司名称，并对任何今天需要答案的教练保持怀疑。真正的自由职业收入来自技能和客户，而不是来自承诺自行运行的项目。

The Federal Trade Commission has kept up a law enforcement sweep it calls Operation AI Comply, targeting business-opportunity schemes that promise easy passive income from artificial intelligence. Cases tied to deceptive AI and ecommerce pitches have wiped out more than $74 million in consumer losses, according to agency filings. The targets are exactly the kind of people who read this site, aspiring solo earners looking for a side hustle. That makes the FTC’s actions a useful map of the traps to avoid before you hand money to any done-for-you AI business. The sweep goes after operations that market AI as a shortcut to guaranteed income, often through automated online storefronts or resale schemes. In one case, the FTC said a business-opportunity operation running under names including Ascend Ecom defrauded consumers of at least $25 million with promises of thousands of dollars a month in passive income. Another action produced an $18 million judgment and a ban against a company called Air AI, which the agency said claimed its technology could replace human salespeople and help people earn tens of thousands of dollars in days. Individual losses in some of these schemes reached into the six figures. Side hustles built around AI tools are booming, and most are legitimate. That growth is also cover for bad actors, since a real trend makes a fake pitch easier to believe. For a freelancer or first-time solopreneur, the damage is not only the upfront fee. These schemes often push people toward business loans, credit lines, or inventory purchases, which can leave a would-be entrepreneur in debt before earning a dollar. Treat any guaranteed-income or automated AI business as a red flag, especially when it comes with high-pressure sales tactics and a high upfront cost. The FTC’s common thread across these cases is bold income promises, inflated testimonials, and fees paid before any real revenue appears. Do your own math before paying. Ask for verifiable earnings claims in writing, search the company name alongside words like complaint and FTC, and be skeptical of any coach who needs an answer today. Real freelance income comes from skills and clients, not from a program that promises to run itself. For readers weighing an AI-based side gig, our look at how Gen Z is building AI side hustles shows what sustainable versions of this work actually involve. Expect the FTC to keep filing cases as AI hype draws more operators into the business-opportunity space. New enforcement actions and refunds to defrauded consumers are likely to follow the ones already announced. Watch for state attorneys general to join in, since they often mirror federal sweeps with their own suits.

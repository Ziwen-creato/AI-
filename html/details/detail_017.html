OpenAI 即将面向中小型广告商推出

Digiday2026-08-10生成于 2026-08-11 17:43

想知道 OpenAI 的广告业务走向何方？检查其职位列表。接下来是规模较小的广告商。

根据 Digiday 审查的六份实时招聘信息，该平台目前正在建立一个专门的中小企业广告部门。这些角色涵盖增长、数据科学、需求策略和销售运营。

该职位的最高层是旧金山的“中小企业广告增长首席数据科学家”职位，薪酬为 29.3 万至 51.5 万美元。这项工作是从头开始为小型广告商构建分析功能，涵盖定位、渠道优化和预测。该列表需要来自 Meta、Google 或 LinkedIn 的候选人。换句话说，OpenAI 正在招聘已经知道如何做到这一点的人，因为他们已经在其他地方做过了。

下面是 SMB 广告增长主管，薪酬为 28.4 万美元到 31.5 万美元，下面是增长营销经理，薪酬为 17.7 万美元到 19.6 万美元，两人都在旧金山。根据其工作规范，第一个角色将在 OpenAI 的广告平台上“构建和扩展小型企业如何发现、采用和发展”，而根据该帖子，第二个角色是让中小型广告商从“兴趣和注册转向随着时间的推移首次有意义的支出和保留”。与数据科学家角色一样，OpenAI 希望招募曾在 Google、Meta、TikTok、Amazon 和 Reddit 广告团队工作过的人员。

然后是都柏林。 OpenAI 正在招聘一名负责中小企业销售的高级供应商经理，但没有列出薪酬。这项工作是负责 OpenAI 外包 SMB 销售供应商的绩效— 代表 OpenAI 处理入站和出站销售、API 销售和其他工作的外部公司。该帖子直言不讳地表示，这并不是为了管理与这些供应商的关系。这是要让他们承担责任：定期进行绩效评估，建立记分卡，并对表现不佳的供应商制定纠正行动计划。

简单来说，OpenAI 不一定拥有庞大的中小企业销售团队。相反，它将向外部供应商支付费用以完成这项工作并雇用某人来监督他们。这与谷歌和 Meta 多年来运行的模式相同。

另外两个位于旧金山的角色使这幅图画更加完整。

其中一位是高级中端市场和中小企业需求策略师，薪酬为 26.6 万至 29.5 万美元。该团队位于 B2B 营销部门内，该团队与增长角色所隶属的 SMB 广告营销团队是不同的团队。策略师的工作是同时为中端市场和中小企业客户生成需求。

另一位是中小型企业的分析工程师，薪酬为 22 万至 33.5 万美元。此人将加入 OpenAI 上市组织内的一个全新数据团队。该帖子称自己是该团队的“早期成员”，这告诉你一些事情：该业务的数据基础设施尚不存在。无论谁得到这份工作，都是白手起家。

对于 OpenAI 的广告业务来说，有趣的举动很少。这就是速度。最终总会发生追逐较小的广告商的情况。它们是任何持久发展的广告业务的支柱——是实现可持续发展的必备条件企业广告商的必备品。以元为例。摩根士丹利 2024 年的一项调查发现，75% 的中小企业使用 Facebook 进行广告投放。从那时起，Meta 就大力依靠人工智能来尽可能多地实现广告自动化。可以肯定的是，中小企业的数量只会增加。

eMarketer 营销和商务人工智能首席分析师 Nate Elliott 表示：“这取决于你相信谁的估计，中小企业占了 Google 和 Meta 广告收入的大部分。” “任何稍微雄心勃勃的广告业务每年都必须从这些较小的广告商那里收取数十亿美元的资金，而 ChatGPT 如果不是雄心勃勃的话，那就什么也不是。”

OpenAI 没有回应 Digiday 的置评请求。

Want to know where OpenAI’s ads business is headed? Check its job listings. Smaller advertisers are next. The platform is currently building out a dedicated SMB ads unit, according to six live job postings reviewed by Digiday. The roles span growth, data science, demand strategy and sales operations. At the top of that stack is a “Lead Data Scientist for SMB Ads Growth” role in San Francisco, paying $293K to $515K. The job is to build the analytics function for smaller advertisers from scratch, spanning targeting, funnel optimization and forecasting. The listing wants candidates from Meta, Google or LinkedIn. In other words, OpenAI is hiring people who already know how to do this because they’ve done it somewhere else. Below that sits a Growth Lead for SMB Ads, paying $284K to $315K, and a Growth Marketing Manager underneath them, paying $177K to $196K, both in San Francisco. The first role, according to its job spec will “build and scale how small businesses discover, adopt and grow” on OpenAI’s ad platform, while the second, according to the posting, is to get SMB advertisers to move from “interest and sign-up to first meaningful spend & retention over time”. Like the data scientist role, OpenAI wants people who’ve worked on the ads teams at Google, Meta, TikTok, Amazon and Reddit. Then there’s Dublin. OpenAI is hiring a Senior Vendor Manager for SMB Sales there, though no compensation is listed. The job is to own performance across OpenAI’s outsourced SMB sales vendors — the outside firms handling inbound and outbound sales, API sales and other work on OpenAI’s behalf. The posting is blunt that this isn’t about managing relationships with those vendors. It’s about holding them to account: running regular performance reviews, building scorecards and putting underperforming vendors on corrective action plans. In simple terms, OpenAI won’t necessarily have a large SMB sales team. Rather, it’s going to pay outside vendors to do that work and hire someone to oversee them. It’s the same model Google and Meta have run for years. Two more San Francisco-based roles round out the picture. One is a Senior Mid-Market & SMB Demand Strategist, paying $266K to $295K. This sits inside B2B Marketing, which is a different team from the SMB Ads Marketing group the growth roles report into. The strategist’s job is to run demand generation for both mid-market and SMB customers at once. The other is an Analytics Engineer for SMB, paying $220K to $335K. This one is joining a brand-new data team inside OpenAI’s go-to-market organization. The posting calls itself an “early member” of that team, which tells you something: the data infrastructure for this business doesn’t exist yet. Whoever gets this job is building it from nothing. With OpenAI’s ads business, it’s rarely the move that’s interesting. It’s the speed. Going after smaller advertisers was always going to happen eventually. They’re the backbone of any ads business built to last — the must-have to enterprise advertisers’ nice-to-have. Take Meta. A Morgan Stanley survey back in 2024 found 75% of SMBs used Facebook for advertising. Since then, Meta has leaned hard on AI to automate as much of that advertising as possible. It’s a fair bet that SMB number has only gone up. “Depending on whose estimates you believe, SMBs make up the majority of ad revenue for Google and Meta,” said eMarketer’s principal analyst, AI in marketing and commerce, Nate Elliott. “Any slightly ambitious ad business will have to collect billions upon billions from these smaller advertisers every year, and ChatGPT is nothing if not ambitious.” OpenAI did not respond to Digiday’s request for comment.

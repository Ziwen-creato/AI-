Local Services Advertising Is Becoming More Integrated With Google Ads

The Online Advantages Mesa SEO service update also comes as Google begins changing how certain Local Services advertisers manage and run campaigns.

Beginning in August 2026, Google says it is starting a phased transition of select U.S. home and storefront service advertisers to Performance Max campaigns with pay-per-lead goals. Initial categories identified by Google include plumbing, HVAC, electrical, appliance repair, house cleaning, lawn care, roofing, pest control and moving.

For local service businesses, Online Advantages Mesa SEO believes these changes further demonstrate why paid local lead generation should not operate independently from the rest of a company's digital presence.

聊天生成模型的被动收入计划

提示：如何才能建立每月 500 美元的被动收入流？具体的分步计划是什么？

人工智能 (AI) 聊天机器人表示：“如果某人一开始只有很少或根本没有被动收入，那么最快的路径通常不是投资，而是创造或收购能够产生经常性收入的资产。”

ChatGPT 建议构建一款数字产品，一旦构建完毕，只需最少的持续工作即可继续盈利。

以下是开始的具体步骤。

1. 选择一个问题

“专注于人们积极想要解决的一个狭窄的、具体的问题。” ChatGPT 建议研究预算、膳食计划、简历撰写、购房清单等主题。

2. 创建一个简单的产品

“创造一些可以节省人们时间或帮助他们取得成果的东西，”聊天机器人说

3. 卖掉它

“使用简单的平台，如 Gumroad、Lemon Squeezy、Etsy（用于数字下载）或您自己的网站。19 至 49 美元的价格对于许多第一批产品来说效果很好，”ChatGPT 说。

4. 创建免费内容

ChatGPT 建议您在受众已经花费时间的地方持续发布。常见平台包括 TikTok、Instagram Reels、YouTube Shorts 和 LinkedIn。您可以展示一个常见错误，然后提及您的产品。

5. 建立电子邮件列表

下一步是提供与您的付费产品相关的免费资源，以便您可以收集电子邮件地址，以便您可以继续接触感兴趣的人，而不必仅依赖社交媒体算法。

6. 根据反馈进行改进

最后一步是根据反馈改进您的产品，因为更高的转化率可以增加收入，而无需更多流量。

如果您销售 25 美元的产品，则每月销售 20 次即可赚取 500 美元。以 50 美元的价格计算，每月仅售出 10 份。

ChatGPT 强调，这些目标通常比试图吸引数十万浏览量更容易实现。

人工智能生成的计划有以下结论。

“最成功的被动收入流通常始于创造长期资产的主动工作。无论是数字产品、视频库还是投资组合，目标都是建立一些能够在最初的努力完成后很长时间内继续产生价值和收入的东西。”

这个被动收入计划的可行性如何？

注册理财规划师（CFP）、雅各布·拜尔财富管理创始人雅各布·拜尔（Jacob Bayer）分享了对这一被动收入计划的以下见解。

该框架对数字业务有意义

拜耳承认，这是数字业务的坚实框架，因为这些步骤提供了推出数字产品所需的真实情况，从识别问题到建立电子邮件列表。

该计划并不完全是被动的

拜耳表示，最大的问题是将这些步骤错误地标记为被动收入。 “它之所以成为副业而不是被动收入，是因为所有正在进行的工作，”他补充道。

不保证转换

根据拜耳的说法，销售漏斗可转化 1% 至 3% 的活跃用户。这意味着，如果您想实现每月 20 笔销售额，则每月必须达到数千名活跃用户。这里最大的障碍是你必须发布病毒式传播的内容，因为无法保证一致的内容会获得关注。

聊天生成模型忽略了重要的财务考虑

拜耳指出，ChatGPT 忽视了关键的财务考虑因素。例如，您可能最终一个月的收入为 0 美元，并且您还忽略了每个季度应缴纳的自营职业税。

计划可能会产生错误的期望

拜耳认为，这一计划只对极少数能够坚持至少 6 到 12 个月构建数字产品的人来说是可行的。您还必须考虑机会成本，因为有更可靠的选择，例如最大化您的退休金或增加您的计费时间，而不是从事 12 周结果不确定的无薪工作。

他总结道：“计划本身不是问题；问题在于它所设定的期望。”

According to a new UBS Global Wealth Report, 440,000 new millionaires were created last year in the United States. If you want to build wealth, you’ll need to focus on generating passive income and growing your portfolio. This is why we prompted ChatGPT to provide a step-by-step plan to build a $500 monthly passive income stream. We then had a real financial advisor review the feasibility of such a plan. Find out more below. Prompt: How can someone build a $500-per-month passive income stream? What's the exact step-by-step plan? “If someone starts with little or no existing passive income, the fastest path usually isn't investing — it's creating or acquiring an asset that generates recurring revenue,” the artificial intelligence (AI) chatbot said. ChatGPT recommended building a digital product that can continue earning with minimal ongoing work once built. Below are the exact steps to get started. “Focus on a narrow, specific problem that people actively want to solve.” ChatGPT suggested looking into topics like budgeting, meal planning, resume writing, home-buying checklists and so on. “Create something that saves people time or helps them achieve a result,” the chatbot said “Use a simple platform such as Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, Etsy (for digital downloads) or your own website. $19 to $49 works well for many first products,” ChatGPT said. ChatGPT recommended that you publish consistently where your audience already spends time. Common platforms include TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and LinkedIn. You can show a common mistake and then mention your product. The next step is to offer a free resource related to your paid product so that you can collect email addresses so you can continue reaching interested people without relying only on social media algorithms. The final step is to improve your product based on feedback, as higher conversion can increase income without requiring more traffic. If you sell a $25 product, you can make $500 per month with 20 sales. At a $50 price point, that’s only 10 sales per month. ChatGPT emphasized that those targets are often more achievable than trying to attract hundreds of thousands of views. The AI-generated plan had the following conclusion. “The most successful passive income streams usually start as active work that creates a long-lived asset. Whether it's a digital product, a library of videos or an investment portfolio, the goal is to build something that can continue generating value — and income — long after the initial effort is complete.” Jacob Bayer, certified financial planner (CFP) and founder of Jacob Bayer Wealth Management, shared the following insights on this passive income plan. Bayer acknowledged that this is a solid framework for a digital business since the steps provide a realistic picture of what it takes to launch a digital product, from identifying the problem to building an email list. The biggest problem is mislabeling the steps as passive income, according to Bayer. “The very reason it’s a side business and not passive income is because of all the ongoing work for it,” he added. A sales funnel converts 1% to 3% of engaged users, per Bayer. This means that if you want to achieve 20 sales per month, you have to reach thousands of engaged users months. The biggest hurdle here is that you have to publish content that goes viral, since there are no guarantees that consistent content will gain traction. Bayer pointed out that ChatGPT ignores crucial financial considerations. For example, you could end up having a month with $0 income and you’re also ignoring self-employment taxes that you would owe every quarter. Bayer believes that this plan is only feasible for a very small minority of people who can remain persistent for at least six to 12 months in building out a digital product. You also have to factor in opportunity cost since there are more reliable options like maxing out your retirement contributions or increasing your billable hours than engaging in 12 weeks of unpaid work with uncertain outcomes.
